WHAT WE WILL NOT SELL YOU

We are not going to tell you we see everything.

Nobody can honestly promise complete coverage, and a vendor who implies it is selling you their confidence rather than their work. What is on offer is a selection you can look at, argue with, and check.

STATE OF THE MEASUREMENT

What we are publishing, and what we are holding back.

◢ DELIVERY — PROVEN

Five consecutive daily runs have been produced and delivered, each with a receipt: a generation record, a stable configuration hash, and post-send message identifiers. No fetch failures. The cadence has held to the second.

◢ SELECTION QUALITY — NOT CLAIMED

During those runs we found a defect in our own scoring: an unauthenticated remote code execution flaw carrying the maximum industry severity rating was placed in our lowest attention class. That is a fault in how we weight, and we found it ourselves.

◢ THE DISCARD NUMBERS — HELD

We intend to publish how much we throw away each day, because that is the number nobody else shows. We are not publishing it yet. A discard rate produced by a scorer we do not trust is a measurement about nothing, and shipping it would be worse than waiting.

◢ WHY THIS IS ON THE PAGE

Because you would have no way of knowing otherwise. Publishing the defect on the day we launch costs us the strongest number we have — and it is the only version of this page we would believe if someone else wrote it.

We do not claim to see everything. We claim to publish what we selected, what we dropped, and the rule that dropped it — starting with the rule that is currently wrong.
